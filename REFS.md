# References on Knotted Surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^4$ and $S^4$

## 1. Existing Tables, Censuses, and Low-Complexity Classifications

- **Yoshikawa (1994)** — K. Yoshikawa, *"An enumeration of surfaces in four-space"*, Osaka J. Math. **31**(3) (1994), 497–522. [Project Euclid](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/osaka-journal-of-mathematics/volume-31/issue-3/An-enumeration-of-surfaces-in-four-space/ojm/1200785461.full) | [OCU Repository PDF](https://www.i-repository.net/contents/osakacu/sugaku/111F0000002-03103-2.pdf)  
  Original enumeration of all **23 weakly prime non-splittable surface-links** (orientable and non-orientable, connected and disconnected, up to orientation and mirror) with $\operatorname{ch}(F) \le 10$, together with $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^4 \setminus F)$ and first elementary ideals $E_1$ (Table II).
- **Yoshikawa (1998)** — K. Yoshikawa, *"The order of a meridian of a knotted Klein bottle"*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. **126**(12) (1998), 3727–3731.  
  Proves the meridian of a knotted Klein bottle in $\mathbb{R}^4$ can have any positive even order $2m$ (smooth irreducibility of the order-4 example was proved by J. Meier, *"Irreducible knotted Klein bottles"*, [arXiv:2510.06358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06358)).
- **T. Yasuda (2001, 2018), Kanenobu (2020), & Kanenobu–Sumi (2018–2020)** — T. Yasuda, *"Crossing-change on ribbon presentations of ribbon 2-knots"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **10**(7) (2001), 929–938; *"Crossing and base numbers of ribbon 2-knots"*, JKTR **10**(7) (2001), 999–1003; *"Ribbon 2-knots of ribbon crossing number four"*, JKTR **27**(10) (2018), 1850058; T. Kanenobu, *"Classification of ribbon 2-knots with ribbon crossing number up to four"*, RIMS Kôkyûroku **2163** (2020), 1–14; T. Kanenobu and T. Sumi, *"Classification of a family of ribbon 2-knots with trivial Alexander polynomial"*, Commun. Korean Math. Soc. **33**(2) (2018), 591–604; *"Classification of ribbon 2-knots presented by virtual arcs with up to four crossings"*, JKTR **28**(10) (2019), 1950067; *"Twisted Alexander polynomial of a ribbon 2-knot of 1-fusion"*, Osaka J. Math. **57**(4) (2020), 789–803.  
  Complete enumeration and classification of **ribbon 2-knots** up to ribbon crossing number $r\text{-cr} \le 4$ (17 knots for $r\text{-cr} \le 3$; 112 for $r\text{-cr} = 4$ after Kanenobu 2020 identified one missed knot $Y_{112}$) and virtual-arc presentations with $\le 4$ crossings, distinguished via Alexander polynomials, twisted Alexander polynomials, and $\operatorname{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ trace sets.
- **Kanenobu–Komatsu (2017) & Kanenobu–Takahashi (2021)** — T. Kanenobu and Y. Komatsu, *"Ribbon 2-knots with symmetric union presentations"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **26**(11) (2017), 1750067; T. Kanenobu and K. Takahashi, *"Classification of ribbon 2-knots of 1-fusion with length up to six"*, Topology Appl. **301** (2021), 107521.  
  Tabulation of oriented ribbon 2-knots with symmetric union presentations and 1-fusion ribbon presentations of length $\le 6$.
- **Jumpei Yasuda (2024, 2025)** — J. Yasuda, *"Computation of the knot symmetric quandle and its application to the plat index of surface-links"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **33**(03) (2024), 2450005, [arXiv:2308.14488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14488); *"On the classification of 2-plat 2-knots"*, [arXiv:2506.15401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15401).  
  Computes knot symmetric quandles from plat presentations of surface-links, and gives a complete classification and table (for determinant $p \le 19$) of oriented **2-plat 2-knots** $F(p, q)$ (plat closures of 2D 4-braids; all 1-fusion ribbon), using determinant and a torsion-like invariant $\tau(F)$ that detects non-invertibility.
- **Satoh (2000) & Ichimori–Kanenobu (2012)** — S. Satoh, *"Virtual knot presentation of ribbon torus-knots"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **9**(4) (2000), 531–542; A. Ichimori and T. Kanenobu, *"Ribbon torus knots presented by virtual knots with up to four crossings"*, JKTR **21**(13) (2012), 1240011.  
  Defines the **Tube map** $\operatorname{Tube}(K)$ from virtual/welded knots to ribbon $T^2$-knots in $\mathbb{R}^4$, and classifies ribbon torus-knots presented by virtual knots with up to four crossings.
- **Kamada (2002) & Nagase–Shima (2023)** — S. Kamada, *Braid and Knot Theory in Dimension Four*, Math. Surveys Monogr. **95**, AMS (2002); T. Nagase and A. Shima, *"Distinguishing surface-links with 4-charts with 2 crossings and 8 black vertices"*, [arXiv:2304.05532](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05532).  
  Foundational text on 2-dimensional braids and **chart descriptions** (including simple ribbon 2-knots from charts with 0 white vertices and small braid index), together with structure and classification results on low-crossing minimal 4-charts.
- **Meier–Zupan (2017, 2018)** — J. Meier and A. Zupan, *"Bridge trisections of knotted surfaces in $S^4$"*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **369**(10) (2017), 7343–7386, [arXiv:1507.08370](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08370).  
  Introduces $(b; c_1, c_2, c_3)$-bridge trisections and **tri-plane diagrams**; proves every surface with bridge number $b \le 3$ is smoothly unknotted.
- **Gong–Lewis-Monkman–Osnes (2026)** — S. Gong, S. Lewis-Monkman, and J. Osnes, *"The 2-Twist Spun Trefoil has Crossing Number Six"*, [arXiv:2606.03799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03799).  
  Proves every 2-knot with tri-plane crossing number $c_{\text{tri}}(K) \le 5$ is ribbon (Theorem 3.3), determines the exact tri-plane crossing number $c_{\text{tri}}(10_2) = 6$ (Corollary 3.5), and conjectures $c_{\text{tri}}(8_1) = 6$ (Conjecture 3.6) and that $\{0_1, 8_1, 10_2\}$ are the only 2-knots with $c_{\text{tri}} \le 6$ (Conjecture 3.7).
- **Naoe (2024)** — H. Naoe, *"Shadows of 2-knots and complexity"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **24**(8) (2024), [arXiv:2208.09173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09173) | [MSP PDF](https://msp.org/agt/2024/24-8/agt-v24-n8-p15-p.pdf).  
  Proves $0_1$ is the unique 2-knot of shadow-complexity $0$, while shadow-complexity $1$ already contains an infinite family $K_n$ ($n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}$, with $K_{-1} = 8_1$ and $K_1 = 9_1$).
- **Kim (2020)** — J. Kim, *"An enumeration of immersed surface-links in 4-space"*, Topology Appl. **279** (2020), 107241.  
  Extends Yoshikawa's method to *immersed* surface-links with singular double points up to $\operatorname{ch}_s \le 9$.

## 2. Diagrammatic Representations, 1-Handle Calculus, & Complete Move Calculi

- **Kawauchi–Shibuya–Suzuki (1982)** — A. Kawauchi, T. Shibuya, and S. Suzuki, *"Descriptions on surfaces in four-space I: Normal forms"*, Math. Sem. Notes Kobe Univ. **10** (1982), 75–125.  
  Establishes hyperbolic splittings (all minima at $t=-1$, saddles at $t=0$, maxima at $t=+1$) and marked graph (`ch`) cross-sections.
- **Swenton (2001) & Kearton–Kurlin (2008)** — F. J. Swenton, *"On a calculus for 2-knots and surfaces in 4-space"*, JKTR **10**(8) (2001), 1133–1141; C. Kearton and V. Kurlin, *"All 2-dimensional links in 4-space live inside a universal 3-dimensional polyhedron"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **8**(3) (2008), 1223–1247, [arXiv:0801.3647](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.3647).  
  Proves Yoshikawa's Question 2.4 affirmatively: two admissible `ch`-diagrams represent ambient-isotopic surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^4$ iff they are related by the **Yoshikawa moves** ($\Omega_1, \dots, \Omega_8$ and mirrors).
- **Boyle (1988) & Kamada (2014, 2020)** — J. Boyle, *"Classifying 1-handles attached to knotted surfaces"*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **306** (1988), 475–487; S. Kamada, *"Cords and 1-handles attached to surface-knots"*, Bol. Soc. Mat. Mex. **20** (2014), 595–609; S. Kamada, *"Tensor products of quandles and 1-handles attached to surface-links"*, [arXiv:2003.12977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.12977).  
  Classifies orientable and non-orientable 1-handles attached to a connected surface-knot $F_0$ via double cosets of the **full peripheral subgroup** $P \backslash \pi_1(S^4 \setminus \nu(F_0)) / P$ (modulo cord inversion; reducing to $\langle\mu\rangle \backslash \pi_1(S^4 \setminus F_0) / \langle\mu\rangle$ when $P = \langle\mu\rangle$, e.g. for $F_0 \cong S^2$), and studies subtleties for disconnected base surface-links.
- **Roseman (1998)** — D. Roseman, *"Reidemeister-type moves for surfaces in four-dimensional space"*, Banach Center Publ. **42** (1998), 347–380.  
  Complete set of 7 **Roseman moves** on generic broken surface diagrams in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
- **Carter–Saito (1998)** — J. S. Carter and M. Saito, *Knotted Surfaces and Their Diagrams*, Math. Surveys Monogr. **55**, AMS (1998); and J. S. Carter, S. Kamada, and M. Saito, *Surfaces in 4-Space*, Springer (2004).  
  Complete set of 15 **movie moves** on motion pictures, broken sheet reconstructions, and cocycle state sums.
- **Hughes–Kim–Miller (2020)** — M. C. Hughes, S. Kim, and M. Miller, *"Isotopies of surfaces in 4-manifolds via banded unlink diagrams"*, Geom. Topol. **24**(3) (2020), 1519–1569, [arXiv:1804.09169](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09169).  
  Complete move calculus (cup/cap, band slides, band swims, Kirby slides) for **banded unlink diagrams** $(L, B)$ in general 4-manifolds.
- **Jabłonowski (2013, 2015, 2017) & Bae–Carter–Choi–Kim (2014)** — M. Jabłonowski, Topology Appl. **160** (2013), [arXiv:1211.6784](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.6784); [arXiv:1505.07995](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07995) (Remark 3.15 posing $\tau^0(3_1)\#P \stackrel{?}{\cong} \tau^2(3_1)\#P$); J. Korean Math. Soc. **54** (2017), [arXiv:1602.08650](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08650); Y. Bae, J. S. Carter, S. Choi, and S. Kim, [arXiv:1407.6028](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6028).  
  Surface singular braid monoids, explicit $\Omega_7$ marker-parity move rules, and open questions on twist-spun 2-knots stabilized by $\mathbb{R}P^2$.
- **Choi–Nelson (2023)** — S. Choi and S. Nelson, *"Marked Graph Mosaics"*, [arXiv:2301.00287](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00287).  
  Grid/mosaic representation of marked graph diagrams using 16 tile types, computing mosaic bounds and involutory quandle (kei) invariants on Yoshikawa's table.

## 3. Annotations of Yoshikawa's Table & Invariant Computations

- **Cazet (2023) & Satoh (2005)** — N. Cazet, *"On the triple point number of surface-links in Yoshikawa's table"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **32**(05) (2023), [arXiv:2205.11120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11120); S. Satoh, *"No 2-knot has triple point number two or three"*, Osaka J. Math. **42** (2005), 543–556.  
  Determines ribbon/pseudo-ribbon status and compiles/bounds the **triple point number** $t(F)$ for all 23 entries in Yoshikawa's table.
- **Allred et al. (2023)** — W. Allred, M. Aragón, Z. Dooley, A. Goldman, Y. Lei, I. Martinez, N. Meyer, D. Peters, S. Warrander, A. Wright, and A. Zupan, *"Tri-plane diagrams for simple surfaces in $S^4$"*, JKTR **32**(06) (2023), [arXiv:2302.08431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08431).  
  Converts all 23 Yoshikawa `ch`-diagrams into **tri-plane diagrams**, determining minimal **bridge numbers** $b(F)$, upper bounds on **tri-plane crossing number** $c_{\text{tri}}(F)$, and $c_{\text{tri}}(\mathcal{P}_{n,m}) = \max\{1, |n-m|\}$ for unknotted non-orientable surfaces.
- **Joseph–Meier–Miller–Zupan (2022)** — J. Joseph, J. Meier, M. Miller, and A. Zupan, *"Bridge trisections and classical knotted surface theory"*, Pacific J. Math. **319**(2) (2022), 343–369, [arXiv:2112.11557](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11557).  
  Relates bridge trisections to meridional rank $\operatorname{mr}(F) \le \min(c_1, c_2, c_3)$ and normal Euler number $e(F)$ via pairwise tangle writhes.
- **Ishii–Nikkuni–Oshiro (2018)** — A. Ishii, R. Nikkuni, and K. Oshiro, *"On calculations of the twisted Alexander ideals for spatial graphs, handlebody-knots and surface-links"*, Osaka J. Math. **55**(2) (2018), 297–313, [arXiv:1503.07969](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.07969).  
  Computes **twisted Alexander ideals** for Yoshikawa's table and corrects three mistakes across **two** entries (`9_1^{1,-2}` and `10_2^{0,-2}`) of Yoshikawa's Table II.
- **Sanderson (1987) & Carter–Kamada–Saito–Satoh (2001)** — B. J. Sanderson, *"Bordism of links in codimension 2"*, J. London Math. Soc. (2) **35**(2) (1987), 367–376; J. S. Carter, S. Kamada, M. Saito, and S. Satoh, *"A theorem of Sanderson on link bordisms in dimension 4"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **1** (2001), 299–310.  
  Establishes mod-2 double and triple linking invariants of surface-links in $\mathbb{R}^4$.
- **Taniguchi (2025), Aranda et al. (2022, 2024), & Blair et al. (2020)** — M. Taniguchi, *"Estimating the Kirby–Thompson Invariants of Surface-Links in the Yoshikawa Table"*, [arXiv:2511.14695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14695); R. Aranda, P. Pongtanapaisan, S. A. Taylor, and C. Zhang, *"Bounding the Kirby–Thompson invariant of spun knots"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **24** (2024), 3363–3399, [arXiv:2112.02420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02420); R. Aranda, P. Pongtanapaisan, and C. Zhang, *"Bounds for Kirby–Thompson invariants of knotted surfaces"*, [arXiv:2206.02936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02936); R. Blair, M. Campisi, S. A. Taylor, and M. Tomova, *"Kirby–Thompson distance for trisections of knotted surfaces"*, Topology Appl. **275** (2020), 107147, [arXiv:1811.00515](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00515).  
  Defines and bounds the **Kirby–Thompson pants/curve-complex invariants** $L(F)$ and $L^\star(F)$ for knotted surfaces and across Yoshikawa's table.
- **Mochizuki (2003), Ashihara (2012), & Kamada–Kim–Lee (2015)** — T. Mochizuki, *"Some calculations of cohomology groups of finite Alexander quandles"*, J. Pure Appl. Algebra **179**(3) (2003), 287–330; S. Ashihara, *"Calculating the fundamental biquandles of surface-links from their ch-diagrams"*, JKTR **21**(10) (2012), 1250102; S. Kamada, J. Kim, and S. Y. Lee, *"Computations of quandle cocycle invariants of surface-links using marked graph diagrams"*, JKTR **24**(10) (2015), 1540010.  
  Explicit polynomial 3-cocycles on finite Alexander/dihedral quandles (Mochizuki), fundamental biquandle presentations from `ch`-diagrams (Ashihara), and direct combinatorial algorithms to compute quandle cocycle state-sum invariants from `ch`-diagrams (Kamada–Kim–Lee).
- **Kamada–Oshiro (2010)** — S. Kamada and K. Oshiro, *"Homology groups of symmetric quandles and cocycle invariants of links and surface-links"*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **362** (2010), 5501–5527.  
  Extends quandle 3-cocycle invariants to **non-orientable** surface-links via symmetric quandles.
- **Lomonaco (1981)** — S. J. Lomonaco, Jr., *"The homotopy groups of knots I. How to compute the algebraic 2-type"*, Pacific J. Math. **95** (1981), 349–390.  
  Computes the **algebraic 2-type** $(\pi_1(E), \pi_2(E), k \in H^3(\pi_1; \pi_2))$ of a surface complement.
- **Morrison–Walker–Wedrich (2022)** — S. Morrison, K. Walker, and P. Wedrich, *"Invariants of 4-manifolds from Khovanov-Rozansky link homology"*, Geom. Topol. **26**(8) (2022), 3367–3420, [arXiv:1907.12194](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.12194).  
  **Skein lasagna modules** and 4D Khovanov-type invariants of surface exteriors / 2-handlebodies.

## 4. Fibered 2-Knots, 4D Triangulations, & Foundational Results

- **Cappell–Shaneson (1976), Gompf (1991), & Hillman (1989, 2002)** — S. E. Cappell and J. L. Shaneson, *"Some new four-manifolds"*, Ann. of Math. **104** (1976), 61–72; R. E. Gompf, *"Killing the Akbulut–Kirby 4-sphere, with relevance to the Andrews–Curtis and Schoenflies problems"*, Topology **30**(1) (1991), 97–115; J. A. Hillman, *2-Knots and Their Groups*, Australian Mathematical Society Lecture Series, Vol. **5**, Cambridge Univ. Press (1989) (see also *Four-Manifolds, Geometries and Knots*, London Math. Soc. Lecture Note Ser. **295**, Cambridge Univ. Press, 2002).  
  Constructs punctured-$T^3$-fibered Cappell–Shaneson 2-knots with groups $\mathbb{Z}^3 \rtimes_{A_m} \mathbb{Z}$ ($\det(tI - A_0) = t^3 - 2t^2 + t - 1$; Gompf's $A_1$ has $\det(tI - A_1) = t^3 - 3t^2 + 2t - 1$), and classifies 2-knots with torsion-free poly-$\mathbb{Z}$ groups topologically up to Gluck reconstruction (Hillman 1989, Ch. 6 / Theorem 6.11).
- **Budney–Burton–Hillman (2012)** — R. Budney, B. A. Burton, and J. A. Hillman, *"Triangulating a Cappell–Shaneson knot complement"*, Math. Res. Lett. **19**(5) (2012), 1117–1126, [arXiv:1109.3899](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3899).  
  Constructs explicit 2-pentachoron ($f_4 = 2$) ideal triangulations of a Cappell–Shaneson 2-knot complement (with monodromy polynomial $t^3 + t^2 - 1$, $|\Delta(-1)| = 1$).
- **Burke–Burton–Spreer (2026)** — R. Burke, B. A. Burton, and J. Spreer, *"Small Triangulations of 4-Manifolds and the 4-Manifold Census"*, Discrete Comput. Geom. (2026) / SoCG 2025, [arXiv:2412.04768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04768).  
  Complete census of closed orientable 4-manifold triangulations up to $f_4 \le 6$ pentachora.
- **Burke–Burton–Ray–Spreer–Thompson–Zymaris (2026)** — R. Burke, B. A. Burton, A. Ray, J. Spreer, F. Thompson, and O. Zymaris, *"Triangulating Spun 2-Knot Complements"*, [arXiv:2607.05923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05923).  
  Converts ideal 3-manifold triangulations of classical knot complements into ideal 4D triangulations of spun 2-knot complements; proves $f_4(S^4 \setminus 8_1) = 6$ pentachora.
- **Hughes–Kim–Miller–Nahm (2026)** — M. C. Hughes, S. Kim, M. Miller, and G. Nahm, *"An irreducible real projective plane in the 4-sphere"*, [arXiv:2605.12921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12921).  
  Disproves Kinoshita's 1961 conjecture by constructing a smoothly irreducible $\mathbb{R}P^2 \subset S^4$ certified via peripheral kernel obstruction.
- **Conway–Powell (2023) & Conway–Orson–Powell (2023)** — A. Conway and M. Powell, *"Embedded surfaces with infinite cyclic knot group"*, Geom. Topol. **27**(2) (2023), 739–821, [arXiv:2009.13461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13461); A. Conway, P. Orson, and M. Powell, *"Unknotting nonorientable surfaces"*, [arXiv:2306.12305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12305).  
  Topological unknotting criteria for orientable surfaces ($g \ge 3$, $\pi_1 \cong \mathbb{Z}$) and non-orientable surfaces ($\pi_1 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$) in $S^4$.
- **Bayer (1980)** — E. Bayer, *"Factorisation is not unique for higher dimensional knots"*, Comment. Math. Helv. **55** (1980), 583–592.  
  Shows prime decomposition of higher-dimensional knots fails to be unique.
- **Baykur–Sunukjian (2016)** — R. İ. Baykur and N. Sunukjian, *"Knotted surfaces in 4-manifolds and stabilizations"*, J. Topol. **9**(1) (2016), 215–231, [arXiv:1504.04086](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04086).  
  Shows exotic knotted surfaces in 4-manifolds become smoothly isotopic after trivial stabilization.
- **Joseph–Pongtanapaisan (2023)** — J. Joseph and P. Pongtanapaisan, *"Bridge numbers and meridional ranks of knotted surfaces and welded knots"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol., [arXiv:2111.09233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09233).  
  Proves meridional rank is not $(-1)$-additive under connected sum of oriented 2-knots.
- **Miyazawa (2023) & Kuhrman (2025)** — J. Miyazawa, *"A gauge theoretic invariant of embedded surfaces in 4-manifolds and exotic $P^2$-knots"*, [arXiv:2312.02041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02041); J. Kuhrman, [arXiv:2507.03798](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03798).  
  Uses Real Seiberg–Witten theory to construct exotic smooth embeddings $\mathbb{R}P^2 \hookrightarrow S^4$ with $\pi_1 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$.
