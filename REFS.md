# References on Knotted Surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^4$ and $S^4$

## 1. Existing Tables, Censuses, and Low-Complexity Classifications

- **Yoshikawa (1994)** — K. Yoshikawa, *"An enumeration of surfaces in four-space"*, Osaka J. Math. **31**(3) (1994), 497–522. [Project Euclid](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/osaka-journal-of-mathematics/volume-31/issue-3/An-enumeration-of-surfaces-in-four-space/ojm/1200785461.full) | [OCU Repository PDF](https://www.i-repository.net/contents/osakacu/sugaku/111F0000002-03103-2.pdf)  
  Original enumeration of all **23 weakly prime non-splittable surface-links** (orientable and non-orientable, connected and disconnected, up to orientation and mirror) with $\operatorname{ch}(F) \le 10$, together with $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^4 \setminus F)$ and first elementary ideals $E_1$ (Table II).
- **Yasuda (2001, 2018) & Kanenobu–Sumi (2019, 2020)** — T. Yasuda, *"Table of ribbon 2-knots"* / *"Ribbon knots with up to four ribbon crossings"*; T. Kanenobu and T. Sumi, *"Classification of ribbon 2-knots with up to four crossings"* (Osaka J. Math. / J. Knot Theory Ramifications).  
  Complete enumeration and classification of **ribbon 2-knots** up to ribbon crossing number $r\text{-cr} \le 4$ (17 knots for $r\text{-cr} \le 3$; 112 for $r\text{-cr} = 4$, including one knot missed by Yasuda), distinguished via Alexander polynomials, twisted Alexander polynomials, and $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$ trace sets.
- **Kanenobu–Komatsu (2017) & Kanenobu–Takahashi (2021)** — T. Kanenobu and Y. Takahashi, *"Classification of 1-fusion ribbon 2-knots with length up to six"*, Topology Appl. **301** (2021), 107778.  
  Tabulation of oriented ribbon 2-knots represented by virtual arcs / Gauss chord diagrams ($\le 4$ crossings) and 1-fusion ribbon presentations of length $\le 6$.
- **Jumpei Yasuda (2025)** — J. Yasuda, *"Classification of 2-plat 2-knots"*, [arXiv:2506.15401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15401).  
  Complete classification and table (for determinant $p \le 19$) of oriented **2-plat 2-knots** $F(p, q)$ (plat closures of 2D 4-braids; all 1-fusion ribbon), using determinant and a torsion-like invariant $\tau(F)$ that detects non-invertibility.
- **Satoh (2000) & Ichimori–Kanenobu (2012)** — S. Satoh, *"Virtual knot presentation of ribbon torus-knots"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **9**(4) (2000), 531–542.  
  Defines the **Tube map** $\operatorname{Tube}(K)$ from virtual/welded knots to ribbon $T^2$-knots in $\mathbb{R}^4$, linking virtual knot censuses (Green, Bar-Natan) to tables of ribbon tori.
- **Kamada (2002) & Nagase–Shima (2018–2023)** — S. Kamada, *Braid and Knot Theory in Dimension Four*, Math. Surveys Monogr. **95**, AMS (2002).  
  Foundational text on 2-dimensional braids and **chart descriptions**; includes a census of simple ribbon 2-knots from charts with 0 white vertices and small braid index.
- **Meier–Zupan (2017, 2018)** — J. Meier and A. Zupan, *"Bridge trisections of knotted surfaces in $S^4$"*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **369**(10) (2017), 7343–7386, [arXiv:1507.08625](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08625).  
  Introduces $(b; c_1, c_2, c_3)$-bridge trisections and **tri-plane diagrams**; proves every surface with bridge number $b \le 3$ is smoothly unknotted.
- **Gong–Lewis-Monkman–Osnes (2026)** — S. Gong, T. Lewis-Monkman, and K. Osnes, *"The 2-Twist Spun Trefoil has Crossing Number Six"*, [arXiv:2606.03799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03799).  
  Proves every 2-knot with tri-plane crossing number $c_{\text{tri}}(K) \le 5$ is ribbon, determines the exact tri-plane crossing number $c_{\text{tri}}(10_2) = 6$ (the first exact nontrivial value), and conjectures $\{0_1, 8_1, 10_2\}$ are the only 2-knots with $c_{\text{tri}} \le 6$.
- **Ishikawa–Koda–Naoe (2024)** — M. Ishikawa, Y. Koda, and H. Naoe, *"2-knots with shadow-complexity at most 1"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **24**(8) (2024), [MSP PDF](https://msp.org/agt/2024/24-8/agt-v24-n8-p15-p.pdf).  
  Proves $0_1$ is the unique 2-knot of shadow-complexity $0$, while shadow-complexity $1$ already contains an infinite family $K_n$ ($n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}$, with $K_{-1} = 8_1$ and $K_1 = 9_1$).
- **Kim (2020)** — J. Kim, *"An enumeration of immersed surface-links in $\mathbb{R}^4$"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications (2020).  
  Extends Yoshikawa's method to *immersed* surface-links with singular double points up to $\operatorname{ch}_s \le 9$.

## 2. Diagrammatic Representations & Complete Move Calculi

- **Kawauchi–Shibuya–Suzuki (1982)** — A. Kawauchi, T. Shibuya, and S. Suzuki, *"Descriptions on surfaces in four-space I: Normal forms"*, Math. Sem. Notes Kobe Univ. **10** (1982), 75–125.  
  Establishes hyperbolic splittings (all minima at $t=-1$, saddles at $t=0$, maxima at $t=+1$) and marked graph (`ch`) cross-sections.
- **Swenton (2001) & Kearton–Kurlin (2008)** — F. J. Swenton, *"On a calculus for 2-knots and surfaces in 4-space"*, JKTR **10**(8) (2001), 1133–1141; C. Kearton and V. Kurlin, *"All 2-dimensional links in 4-space live inside a universal 3-dimensional polyhedron"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. **8**(3) (2008), 1223–1247, [arXiv:0802.2775](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.2775).  
  Proves Yoshikawa's Question 2.4 affirmatively: two admissible `ch`-diagrams represent ambient-isotopic surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^4$ iff they are related by the 8 **Yoshikawa moves** ($\Omega_1, \dots, \Omega_8$ and mirrors).
- **Roseman (1998)** — D. Roseman, *"Reidemeister-type moves for surfaces in four-dimensional space"*, Banach Center Publ. **42** (1998), 347–380.  
  Complete set of 7 **Roseman moves** on generic broken surface diagrams in $\mathbb{R}^3$ (double point curves, branch points, triple points).
- **Carter–Saito (1998)** — J. S. Carter and M. Saito, *Knotted Surfaces and Their Diagrams*, Math. Surveys Monogr. **55**, AMS (1998); and J. S. Carter, S. Kamada, and M. Saito, *Surfaces in 4-Space*, Springer (2004).  
  Complete set of 15 **movie moves** on motion pictures, broken sheet reconstructions, and cocycle state sums.
- **Hughes–Kim–Miller (2020)** — M. C. Hughes, S. Kim, and M. Miller, *"Isotopies of surfaces in 4-manifolds via banded unlink diagrams"*, Geom. Topol. **24**(3) (2020), 1519–1569, [arXiv:1804.09169](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09169).  
  Complete move calculus (cup/cap, band slides, band swims, Kirby slides) for **banded unlink diagrams** $(L, B)$ in general 4-manifolds.
- **Jablonowski (2013, 2017)** — M. Jabłonowski, *"On a surface singular braid monoid"*, Topology Appl. **160** (2013), [arXiv:1211.6784](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.6784); *"Presentations and representations of surface singular braid monoids"*, J. Korean Math. Soc. **54** (2017), [arXiv:1602.08650](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08650).  
  Encodes marked graph diagrams as closed words in the **surface singular braid monoid**, giving explicit words for all 23 surface-links in Yoshikawa's table. Also studies hard surface-unlinks.
- **Choi–Nelson (2022)** — J. Choi and S. Nelson, *"Marked Graph Mosaics"*, [arXiv:2301.00287](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00287).  
  Grid/mosaic representation of marked graph diagrams using 16 tile types, computing mosaic bounds and involutory quandle (kei) invariants on Yoshikawa's table.

## 3. Annotations of Yoshikawa's Table & Invariant Computations

- **Cazet (2022)** — N. Cazet, *"On the triple point number of surface-links in Yoshikawa's table"*, J. Knot Theory Ramifications **32**(05) (2023), [arXiv:2205.11120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11120) ([HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.11120v1)).  
  Determines ribbon/pseudo-ribbon status and compiles/bounds the **triple point number** $t(F)$ for all 23 entries in Yoshikawa's table (exact for 21 entries; $t(10_2^{0,-2}) \le 10$ and $2 \le t(10_1^{-1,-1}) \le 12$), building on Satoh (2001), Satoh–Shima (2004, 2005), and Kamada–Oshiro (2009).
- **Allred et al. & Zupan (2023)** — W. Allred, M. Aragón, Z. Dooley, A. Goldman, Y. Lei, I. Martinez, N. Meyer, D. Peters, S. Warrander, A. Wright, and A. Zupan, *"Tri-plane diagrams for simple surfaces in $S^4$"*, JKTR **32**(06) (2023), [arXiv:2302.08431](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.08431).  
  Converts all 23 Yoshikawa `ch`-diagrams into **tri-plane diagrams**, proving exact minimal **bridge numbers** $b(F)$ for all 23 entries, upper bounds on **tri-plane crossing number** $c_{\text{tri}}(F)$, and proving $c_{\text{tri}}(\mathcal{P}_{n,m}) = \max\{1, |n-m|\}$ for unknotted non-orientable surfaces.
- **Joseph–Meier–Miller–Zupan (2022)** — J. Joseph, J. Meier, M. Miller, and A. Zupan, *"Bridge trisections and classical knotted surface theory"*, Pacific J. Math. **319**(2) (2022), 343–369, [arXiv:2105.03523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03523).  
  Relates bridge trisections to meridional rank $\operatorname{mr}(F) \le \min(c_1, c_2, c_3)$ and normal Euler number $e(F)$ via pairwise tangle writhes.
- **Ishii–Nikkuni–Oshiro (2018)** — A. Ishii, R. Nikkuni, and K. Oshiro, *"On calculations of the twisted Alexander ideals for spatial graphs, handlebody-knots and surface-links"*, Osaka J. Math. **55**(2) (2018), 297–313, [arXiv:1503.07969](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.07969).  
  Computes **twisted Alexander ideals** for Yoshikawa's table (separating pairs with identical classical Alexander ideals) and **corrects three calculations in Yoshikawa's Table II**.
- **Taniguchi (2025), Blair et al. (2022), & Aranda et al. (2022, 2024)** — M. Taniguchi, *"Estimating the Kirby–Thompson Invariants of Surface-Links in the Yoshikawa Table"*, [arXiv:2511.14695](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.14695); R. Aranda, P. Pongtanapaisan, S. A. Taylor, and S. Zhang, [arXiv:2112.02420](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.02420) & [arXiv:2206.02936](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.02936).  
  Determines or bounds the **Kirby–Thompson pants/curve-complex invariants** $L(F)$ and $L^\star(F)$ across Yoshikawa's table.
- **Ashihara (2012) & Kamada–Kim–Lee (2015)** — S. Ashihara, *"Calculating the fundamental biquandles of surface-links from their ch-diagrams"*, JKTR **21** (2012); S. Kamada, J. Kim, and S. Y. Lee, *"Computations of quandle cocycle invariants of surface-links using marked graph diagrams"*, JKTR **24**(10) (2015).  
  Direct combinatorial algorithms to compute **fundamental biquandles** and **quandle 3-cocycle state-sum invariants** $\Phi_\theta(F)$ directly from `ch`-diagrams without explicitly constructing a full 3D broken surface diagram.
- **Kamada–Oshiro (2010)** — S. Kamada and K. Oshiro, *"Homology groups of symmetric quandles and cocycle invariants of links and surface-links"*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **362** (2010), 5501–5527.  
  Extends quandle 3-cocycle invariants to **non-orientable** surface-links via symmetric quandles (good involutions), yielding lower bounds on $t(F)$.
- **Lomonaco (1981)** — S. J. Lomonaco, Jr., *"The homotopy groups of knots I. How to compute the algebraic 2-type"*, Pacific J. Math. **95** (1981), 349–390.  
  Computes the **algebraic 2-type** $(\pi_1(E), \pi_2(E), k \in H^3(\pi_1; \pi_2))$ of a surface complement directly from a `ch`-diagram.
- **Morrison–Walker–Wedrich (2022) & Ren–Willis (2024)** — S. Morrison, K. Walker, and P. Wedrich, *"Invariants of 4-manifolds from Khovanov-Rozansky homology"*, Geom. Topol. **26** (2022), [arXiv:1907.12194](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.12194).  
  **Skein lasagna modules** and 4D Khovanov-type invariants of surface exteriors / 2-handlebodies, sensitive to smooth structure beyond $\pi_1$.

## 4. 4D Triangulations, Alternate Complexity Measures, & Foundational Conjectures

- **Budney–Burton–Hillman (2012)** — R. Budney, B. A. Burton, and J. A. Hillman, *"Triangulating Cappell–Shaneson knot complements"*, Math. Res. Lett. **19**(5) (2012), 1117–1126, [arXiv:1108.5961](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5961).  
  Constructs explicit 2-pentachoron ($f_4 = 2$) ideal triangulations of Cappell–Shaneson 2-knot complements in *Regina*.
- **Burke–Burton–Spreer (2026)** — R. Burke, B. A. Burton, and J. Spreer, *"Small Triangulations of 4-Manifolds and the 4-Manifold Census"*, Discrete Comput. Geom. (2026) / SoCG 2025, [arXiv:2412.04768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04768).  
  Complete census of closed orientable 4-manifold triangulations up to $f_4 \le 6$ pentachora, highlighting the 4D Pachner-graph simplification barrier.
- **Burke–Burton–Ray–Spreer–Thompson–Zymaris (2026)** — R. Burke, B. A. Burton, A. Ray, J. Spreer, D. Thompson, and D. Zymaris, *"Triangulating Spun 2-Knot Complements"*, [arXiv:2607.05923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05923).  
  General algorithm converting ideal 3-manifold triangulations of classical knot complements into ideal 4D triangulations of spun 2-knot complements; proves the spun trefoil ($8_1$) has **exact minimum complexity $f_4(S^4 \setminus 8_1) = 6$** pentachora, with upper bounds $f_4 \le 10$ (`5a2`), $f_4 \le 12$ (`7a7`, `8n3`), and $f_4 \le 16$ (`4a1` = $10_1$).
- **Conway–Powell (2023)** — A. Conway and M. Powell, *"Topological unknotting of surfaces in the 4-sphere"*, J. Eur. Math. Soc. (JEMS) **25**(9) (2023), 3701–3740, [arXiv:2009.13426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13426).  
  Proves that closed orientable surfaces $\Sigma_g \subset S^4$ of genus $g \ge 3$ with $\pi_1(S^4 \setminus \Sigma_g) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ are *topologically* unknotted (complementing Freedman 1984 for $g = 0$; $g \in \{1,2\}$ remains open topologically, and *smooth* unknotting remains open for all $g \ge 0$).
- **Baykur–Sunukjian (2016)** — R. İ. Baykur and N. Sunukjian, *"Knotted surfaces in 4-manifolds and stabilizations"*, J. Topol. **9**(1) (2016), 215–231, [arXiv:1309.2543](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.2543).  
  Shows that exotic smoothly knotted surfaces in 4-manifolds become smoothly isotopic after a single *trivial* stabilization ($\# T^2_{\text{std}}$), contrasting with nontrivial internal 1-handle stabilization (Hosokawa–Kawauchi 1979; Boyle 1988).
- **Joseph–Pongtanapaisan (2023)** — J. Joseph and P. Pongtanapaisan, *"Bridge numbers and meridional ranks of knotted surfaces and welded knots"*, Algebr. Geom. Topol., [arXiv:2111.09233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09233).  
  Proves that meridional rank is **not** $(-1)$-additive under connected sum of oriented 2-knots, implying that either the 4D Meridional Rank Conjecture fails or 4D bridge number also fails $(-1)$-additivity on orientable surfaces.
- **Miyazawa (2023) & Kang–Park–Taniguchi (2025)** — J. Miyazawa, *"A gauge theoretic invariant of embedded surfaces in 4-manifolds and exotic $P^2$-knots"*, [arXiv:2312.02041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02041); S. Kang, J. Park, and M. Taniguchi, *"More exotic $\mathbb{R}P^2$-knots and homotopy spheres"* (2025).  
  Uses Real Seiberg–Witten theory to construct infinitely many **exotic smooth embeddings $\mathbb{R}P^2 \hookrightarrow S^4$** (topologically unknotted with $\pi_1 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, smoothly knotted, and linked to Gluck twists on Montesinos roll-spins).
